# 90-day public pilot

## Days 0-15
Freeze the charter, schemas, synthetic cases and forbidden uses. Recruit one affected-world reviewer and one independent critic.

## Days 16-35
Run 20 synthetic and historical process windows. Calibrate false positive/negative cases for zombie, parasite and domination states.

## Days 36-60
Pilot one low-risk domain: research governance, open-source maintenance or organizational handoff. No employment, healthcare, welfare, policing or legal eligibility decisions.

## Days 61-90
Publish:

- all rule changes;
- counterexamples;
- one failed classification;
- one successful liberation;
- affected-world feedback;
- whether the system itself entered repetition or extractive behavior.

Success is not number of labels. Success is fewer repeated dead loops, more corrected worldlines and more independent successors.
