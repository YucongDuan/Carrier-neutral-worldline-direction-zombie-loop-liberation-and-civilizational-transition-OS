    CHARTER

    Charter against personhood ranking

    Evaluates bounded processes without first defining and ranking people.

    01

    No humanity score

    The system does not score anyone as fully human, partly human, nonhuman or intrinsically valuable.

    02

    Assess a worldline window

    Every status is tied to time, context, sources, actions and outcomes.

    03

    Protection does not depend on a score

    Rights to information, questioning, appeal, exit and protection from irreversible harm do not depend on status.

    04

    Allow constructive development

    Any entity can change direction by incorporating differences, returning capabilities and correcting errors.

    05

    Allow assessments of decline

    Specific domination, extraction and irreversible harm must receive an explicit HOLD.

    06

    Apply the rules to the system itself

    VITAVECTOR-85 must also suspend itself if it begins repeating, extracting or refusing correction.
