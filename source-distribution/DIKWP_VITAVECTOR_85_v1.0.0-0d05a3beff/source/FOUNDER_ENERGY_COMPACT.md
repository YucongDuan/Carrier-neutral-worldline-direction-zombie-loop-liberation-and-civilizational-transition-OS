# Founder energy compact

VITAVECTOR-85 must not become another founder-dependent zombie loop.

- No new feature without an external user, counterexample or maintainer.
- Repeated documentation without new evidence enters `STAGNANT_MAINTENANCE`.
- Two release cycles without world effect trigger scope reduction or archive.
- Public issues replace unlimited private support.
- Every accepted feature must return at least one of: adoption, maintainer capacity, reproducible evidence, paid work, source recognition or future support reduction.
